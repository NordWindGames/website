I am Mathias, a developer from northern Germany, and I am building my first game: **HoldStrong: The Last Tower**.

This devlog covers two things — game development in general, and where my own build actually stands. It is not a marketing blog. Think of it as a workshop notebook that happens to be public.

## What I am building

HoldStrong is a roguelite tower defense set in the far north: one last tower before the Eternal Ice, endless waves, and a death that does not send you back to the start. You fall, you are carried to Valhalla, and you return stronger in the morning.

There is no win state. That is not a missing feature, it is the point — you do not beat the Fimbulwinter, you outlast it.

## Why in public

Because a game gets better when it does not only exist in my head.

I want the reasoning behind decisions to be visible, and I want you to be able to push back on them. So I will ask for feedback at the points where it can still change something, not once everything is locked. If you think a mechanic is boring, the useful moment to say so is now, not at release.

## The plan: weekly

One post a week is the goal.

The hard part is not the writing, it is the rhythm. The weeks where little got finished are exactly the weeks you would rather stay quiet — and that is where devlogs go silent. I would rather post anyway: a thin week is information too, and an honest "this one did not go well" beats saying nothing.

So I will write down what got built, what did not work, and what I learned along the way. Including the parts where I was wrong.

Looking forward to it. Let's start.
